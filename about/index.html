ABOUT THE ACADEMY

Better Fighters. Better Teammates. Future Leaders.

The Academy exists to make learning intentional: preserve useful knowledge, help fighters improve more safely and efficiently, and raise people capable of passing what they learn to the next generation.

WHY WE EXIST

Skill should not depend on luck.

People improve when good experience is paired with clear instruction, useful repetition, honest feedback, and reflection. The Academy exists to make that process easier to find and easier to repeat.

We teach so fighters can become confident and capable, think critically, adapt under pressure, strengthen the community around them, preserve what works, and eventually help someone else grow.

  • Build confident, capable fighters.
  • Develop critical thinking and adaptability.
  • Create a safer, stronger training culture.
  • Preserve useful knowledge and pass it forward.
  • Build future leaders, not permanent dependents.

THE ACADEMY STANDARD

What we value on the field.

A Cadet does not need to be excellent on day one. A Cadet does need to become someone others can safely train beside and trust in a game built on honor.

S

Safety

Protect people before chasing victory. Stop when the field becomes unsafe, control your movement, and match intensity to the people around you.

H

Honor

Call your own shots and deaths. Do not turn disagreement into an argument loop that ruins the field for everyone else.

C

Control

Control your weapon, body, speed, and emotions. Technical skill without restraint is not the Academy standard.

A

Attention

Notice what is happening before rushing to act: range, openings, teammates, hazards, objectives, and how an opponent responds.

R

Reflection

Examine practice honestly. Ask what improved, what repeated, what failed, and what deserves the next focused rep.

G

Growth

Measure progress across practices and months—not one exchange, one tournament, or one bad day.

PEOPLE OF THE ACADEMY

Cadets learn. Preceptors make learning possible.

Titles describe responsibilities inside the training program. They do not create authority over Amtgard rules, park officers, or field officials.

The Learner

Cadets

Cadets are students of the Academy. A beginner is not treated as a failed expert. They are expected to be safe, honest, teachable, willing to practice, and ready to take the next useful step.

A Cadet is a learner under arms.

The Teacher

Preceptors

Preceptors are Academy instructors and field teachers. Their job is not to demonstrate superiority. Their job is to protect safety and enthusiasm, make the lesson understandable, give useful feedback, and help the student become increasingly independent.

The instructor succeeds when the student can continue the work.

THE PRECEPTOR'S CREED

Teach beyond the next fight.

The Academy does not exist to gather followers around one talented fighter. Its purpose is to create a culture where useful knowledge survives the person who first taught it.

I do not seek glory for myself, but growth for my fighters.

I do not create followers, but future leaders.

I do not teach only for today, but for the battles tomorrow.

I forge skill. I build character. I raise Warlords.

The Preceptor's Creed

The Academy is a school, not a parallel government.

The Academy teaches skill, safety, learning habits, and literacy. Amtgard's current Rules of Play governs the game. Dragonspine's Corpora governs applicable Kingdom structure. Reeves, weapon checkers, champions, monarchy, event staff, and other proper officials keep their official responsibilities.
