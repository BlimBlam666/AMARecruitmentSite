TRAINING WITH PURPOSE

Foam fighting becomes more rewarding when practice has direction.

The Academy turns experience into a repeatable learning path: understand the skill, drill it safely, test it under pressure, review what happened, and return stronger.

I Fight Safely

Build control, field awareness, clean calls, and habits that protect the people around you. Safety is not separate from skill—it is the first proof of it.

II Fight Intelligently

Develop footwork, range, defense, timing, reads, planning, and adaptation. The goal is not merely to swing faster, but to understand the exchange.

III Grow Beyond the Bout

Learn teamwork, communication, reflection, field judgment, and eventually the ability to help another fighter improve.

THE FIGHTER TRACK

From first day to field-ready.

The Academy builds from safety and clean fundamentals toward weapon literacy, combat intelligence, and useful team play. Each stage gives the next stage something solid to stand on.

F100

Recruit Foundations

Safety, rules literacy, stance, controlled movement, clean shots, and good practice habits.

F200

Cadet Fundamentals

Footwork, range, defense, offense, recovery, first reads, and structured sparring.

F300

Aspirant Weapon Arts

Practical understanding of major weapon systems and how to fight with or against them.

F400

Combat Intelligence

Timing, reads, baits, feints, planning, adaptation, and calm decision-making under pressure.

F500

Field & Team Combat

Ditch lines, team movement, objectives, command calls, tournament readiness, and final assessment.

THE ACADEMY METHOD

Learn it. Drill it. Use it.

Good instruction does not drown a fighter in ten corrections at once. It gives them one useful idea, a clear way to practice it, and feedback they can act on immediately.

The Academy lesson loop is deliberately simple: see the skill, understand why it matters, practice it correctly, add pressure, and reflect on what changed.

Understand What is the skill for, and what does success look like?
Practice Use controlled repetitions with a clear purpose.
Apply Add sparring, pressure, or a field problem.
Review Name one success, one correction, and the next rep.

MORE THAN WINNING

The fighter we are trying to build.

A Cadet is not expected to arrive excellent. A Cadet is expected to grow into someone the field can trust.

S

Safe

Protect people before chasing victory.

H

Honorable

Call cleanly, take your own deaths, and do not poison the game with argument.

C

Controlled

Control the weapon, body, emotions, and speed.

A

Aware

Notice range, allies, opponents, hazards, and changing conditions.

T

Teachable

Accept useful correction, reflect, and continue the work.
