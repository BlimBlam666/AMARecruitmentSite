THE TRAINING PATH

A path, not a pile of techniques.

Academy training builds from safety and movement into weapon skill, decision-making, teamwork, and independent growth. Each stage gives the next one something solid to stand on.

F100–F500

The Fighter Track.

The Fighter Track is a progressive course structure. It begins with trustworthiness and clean fundamentals, then expands into weapon systems, tactical thinking, and useful field participation.

F100

Recruit Foundations

Learn how to enter the field safely and build clean habits.

  • Field Safety and Honor
  • Amtgard Combat Basics
  • Ready Stance
  • Clean Shot Mechanics
  • Basic Drills and Practice Habits
F200

Cadet Fundamentals

Build purposeful movement, range control, defense, recovery, and first reads.

  • Footwork 101
  • Range 101
  • Basic Blocking
  • Block-Strike Basics
  • Beginner Sparring Lab
F300

Aspirant Weapon Arts

Learn the jobs, strengths, limits, and fundamentals of major weapon systems.

  • Single Sword
  • Sword & Shield
  • Florentine
  • Great Weapon / Polearm
  • Projectile Awareness
F400

Combat Intelligence

Learn to perceive, plan, adapt, and solve the fight instead of repeating habits blindly.

  • Combat Loop & Tempo
  • Hot Reads and Cold Reads
  • Baits and Feints
  • Planning a Bout
  • Post-Fight Analysis
F500

Field & Team Combat

Apply individual skill to lines, objectives, teammates, and field conditions.

  • Ditch Line Fundamentals
  • Small Unit Movement
  • Objective Fighting
  • Command Calls
  • Graduate Trial

COURSE SPOTLIGHTS

What the progression feels like.

The catalogue is broad, but the pattern is simple: trust first, mechanics next, then judgment under increasing pressure.

F101

Field Safety and Honor

The first gate: HOLD calls, controlled movement, clean conduct, and the honor habits that let the rest of training matter.

F201

Footwork 101

Advance, retreat, angle, pivot, and recover with purpose while preserving balance, guard, and awareness.

F301

Single Sword 101

Guard, range, controlled offense, basic defense, and recovery with one weapon and nowhere for bad structure to hide.

F407

Baits 101

Invite a predictable response only when the answer is already prepared—and learn to reset when the opponent refuses the invitation.

F512

The Graduate Trial

A practical review of safety, skill, weapon literacy, thinking, and team usefulness. Readiness matters more than perfection.

HOW A LESSON WORKS

One idea. Useful reps. A clear next step.

Academy instruction is designed to be focused, practical, repeatable, and measurable. The lesson should survive the instructor who first taught it.

DRAGONSPINE CIVIC ARMS

Learn the community you fight in.

The Academy is not limited to sword work. Civic Arms teaches how Dragonspine, its parks, officers, public meetings, service roles, events, awards, and long-term paths fit together.

The goal is not to create procedural combatants. It is to help members participate responsibly, know who holds proper authority, and understand where to find the right answer.

THE GRADUATE STANDARD

Five gates. One question: can the field trust you?

The Graduate Trial is not a crown and not a single duel. It asks whether a Cadet can act safely, apply fundamentals, think clearly, use more than one fighting tool, and contribute to a team without constant direction.

Safety Gate

Controlled shots, immediate HOLD response, clean calls, legal targeting, and emotional control.

Skill Gate

Stance, range, footwork, defense, offense, and recovery under controlled pressure.

Weapon Gate

Competent, safe use of at least two weapon systems and an understanding of their jobs.

Mind Gate

Explain what you observed, what you planned, what changed, and what you would train next.

Field Gate

Communicate, maintain spacing, support allies, pursue the objective, and adapt when the field changes.

Graduate does not mean finished. It means the fighter has shown enough safety, judgment, and independence to continue the work with greater responsibility.

BEGIN AT THE BEGINNING

You do not need to choose your whole path today.

The first useful course is the one that matches the fighter standing in front of us. Start with the first gate. Build from there.
